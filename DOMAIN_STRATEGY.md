## 📊 MECE Framework for Domain Evaluation

### Dimension 1: BRANDABILITY (Mutually Exclusive)
- **Descriptive** (explains what it does)
- **Abstract** (unique word/phrase)
- **Coined** (invented word)
- **Compound** (combines words)

### Dimension 2: MEMORABILITY (Mutually Exclusive)
- **Short** (≤8 characters)
- **Medium** (9-15 characters)
- **Long** (≥16 characters)

### Dimension 3: VIRALITY POTENTIAL (Collectively Exhaustive)
- **Pronunciation** (easy to say)
- **Spelling** (easy to type)
- **Word-of-mouth** (easy to share verbally)
- **Social media** (hashtag-friendly)
- **SEO** (keyword-rich)

## 📈 BEST PRACTICES FOR VIRAL DOMAINS

### 1. **LENGTH MATTERS**
```
Optimal: 5-10 characters (without TLD)
Good: 11-15 characters
Avoid: 16+ characters

Examples:
✅ DubWorld.tv (8 chars) - Perfect
✅ GlobalLive.tv (10 chars) - Good
⚠️ VoiceWorld.tv (10 chars + meaning) - Acceptable
❌ LiveWorldTelevision.com (18 chars) - Too long
```

### 2. **PRONUNCIATION TEST**
**The Radio Test**: Can you say it on the radio and listeners can spell it?

✅ **Pass**: WorldNews.tv, DubWorld.tv, 7Live.tv
⚠️ **Caution**: Omnilive.tv (is it Omni or Omnee?)
❌ **Fail**: Wrld.tv (too ambiguous)

### 3. **SPELLING TEST**
**The Bar Test**: Can you tell someone at a noisy bar and they can find it?

✅ **Pass**: GlobalLive.tv (two common words)
✅ **Pass**: NewsWorld.live (clear spelling)
⚠️ **Caution**: Dublix.tv (is it Dublicks? Dublix?)

### 4. **MEMORABILITY TEST**
**The Second Mention Test**: Will users remember it after one mention?

✅ **Pass**: Short, unique names (DubWorld, 7Live)
⚠️ **Caution**: Generic names (NewsWorld, PlanetTV)

### 5. **BRANDABILITY TEST**
**The Trademark Test**: Can you own it legally and build brand equity?

✅ **Invented words**: Dublix, Omnilive (easier to trademark)
⚠️ **Generic terms**: WorldNews (harder to protect)

### 6. **SEO TEST**
**The Search Test**: Will it rank for relevant searches?

✅ **Keyword-rich**: WorldNews.tv, NewsWorld.live
⚠️ **Branded**: DubWorld.tv (needs SEO work)
❌ **Generic**: Now.tv (too broad)

### 7. **SOCIAL MEDIA TEST**
**The Hashtag Test**: Is it available on social platforms?

Check:
- Twitter/X: @YourDomain
- Instagram: @YourDomain
- TikTok: @YourDomain
- YouTube: @YourDomain

✅ Consistent handles = stronger brand

### 8. **INTERNATIONALIZATION TEST**
**The Global Test**: Does it work in other languages?

✅ **Universal**: 7Live.tv (numbers are universal)
✅ **English-friendly**: DubWorld.tv (English is lingua franca)
⚠️ **Culture-specific**: Avoid slang, puns that don't translate

### 9. **TLD (TOP-LEVEL DOMAIN) SELECTION**

**Recommended for LiveWorldTV**:

**Tier 1** (.tv, .live, .world):
- `.tv` - Perfect for video/television ($40-$50/year)
- `.live` - Great for live streaming ($20-$30/year)
- `.world` - Global positioning ($30-$40/year)

**Tier 2** (.io, .co):
- `.io` - Tech-savvy, startup vibe ($35-$45/year)
- `.co` - Modern, short alternative to .com ($25-$35/year)

**Tier 3** (.com):
- `.com` - Most trusted, but expensive and taken ($10/year if available)

**Avoid**:
- ❌ `.net`, `.org` (confusing for consumers)
- ❌ `.biz`, `.info` (outdated, spammy)
- ❌ Country TLDs unless targeting specific market

### 10. **FUTURE-PROOFING**
**The Pivot Test**: Can the domain grow with your product?

✅ **Flexible**: GlobalLive.tv (can add features beyond dubbing)
✅ **Broad**: WorldNews.tv (can expand to entertainment)
⚠️ **Locked**: DubWorld.tv (tied to dubbing feature)

## 💡 CREATIVE NAMING TECHNIQUES

### 1. **Portmanteau** (Blend two words)
- DubWorld = Dub + World
- Transliv = Translate + Live
- Dublix = Dub + Flix

### 2. **Prefix/Suffix** (Add modern tech affixes)
- Dub + ify = Dubify
- Global + ly = Globally (taken)
- Live + er = Liver (bad)
- Omni + live = Omnilive ✓

### 3. **Compound** (Stack meaningful words)
- Global + Live = GlobalLive ✓
- World + News = WorldNews ✓
- Live + Dub = LiveDub ✓

### 4. **Abbreviation** (Shorten phrases)
- GLN = Global Live News (bad)
- WLT = World Live TV (bad)
- 7L = 7 Live (interesting but ambiguous)

### 5. **Metaphor** (Use imagery)
- Planet, Globe, Earth = Global concept
- Now, Live, Real = Real-time concept
- Voice, Speak, Talk = Dubbing concept
